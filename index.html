Settings

Flip points come from the TechDisc simulator: each disc is thrown at 20 to 85 mph from the reference release, and the chart marks where it stops holding hyzer and where it turns over. Mapping one disc takes at most 6 API calls (about 7 seconds) and only runs when you press a Map button. Results are saved on this device for each release angle.
